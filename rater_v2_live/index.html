How this works about 10 minutes for all 80

You are judging the geometry of a generated serpentine zip, not whether the garment looks good. 80 images, one at a time. Nobody will see your answers and nobody will grade you.

Count curves exactly

A serpentine zip has S-shaped waves. Count each full S-curve (one left-to-right-to-left oscillation) as one. If you see three waves, say three. Exact numbers, no bins.

Measure the swing

Does the zip path lean left, lean right, or stay centred? Pick the dominant direction of the overall path.

Say when you cannot tell

"Cannot determine" is a real answer. A guess is worse than a gap.

Zoom in

The zip teeth and curves are small. Use + and scroll or drag to look closely before you answer.

Question 3 is the visibility gate

If the zip region is too obscured or too small to judge, answer no and the rest is skipped. That is not giving up. It is the most useful thing you can do.

what counts as left, centre, right

centre the path stays roughly centred on the centre-front seam.
right the path curves predominantly toward the wearer's right (viewer's left).
