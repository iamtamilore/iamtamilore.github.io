TermsGuard corpus

The TermsGuard engine pointed at a real, ongoing data-collection problem. Each company's privacy policy is fetched on the schedule below, scored by the same GDPR-cosine pipeline that powers the live demo, and stored versioned with the diff between snapshots. The corpus is the proof that the engine works on documents it has never seen before.

Sampling frame. The corpus covers 17 companies that are either (a) the largest data processors operating under GDPR scope, or (b) among the top five in their EU category. The list is the sample, not a ranking. Each entry justifies itself by size or category, not by score. 14 of 17 fetched successfully this run; 3 were rate-limited or bot-blocked and will retry next run.

What this is and is not

This page is a record of TermsGuard's measurement of the consumer-facing privacy policy each company publishes. It is not a legal audit. It is not a comparison (each company's score is independent). It is not a guarantee of freshness. Companies change their policies without notice. The numbers reflect the policy as it was on the date shown.

The corpus is fetched on a weekly cadence via GitHub Actions. Source: terms-guard-ai on GitHub. The live demo this corpus powers: newtazer-terms-guard-ai.hf.space.
