What a receipt contains

Four records joined by a single task id: what was requested, what came back, what I decided, and what the quality gate measured. If any of those are missing for a call, the row says so rather than showing a zero.

request
model, full prompt, condition, reference URLs, job type
result
state, credits consumed, duration, output hash, fail reason
review
the human keep or reject decision and the reason
qa
embedding similarity, colour distance, OCR confidence, gate pass
