The drift bug — and its fix

The first build (EP_MUSASHI_pre-drift-fix.mp4) shipped with the same caption/picture drift bug that EP_SPEEDMASTER hit. plan_shots.py built the shot list from the spoken-span only — 5.16 seconds of pauses were missing — and the builder rescaled every shot by 1.1479 to fit. The pictures walked ahead of the captions until they were 2.7 seconds out by the end of the video.

7 of 19 shots showed the wrong picture under the caption. Segment 2's frame showed "instead of steel? In 1612," which is two different lines than shot 2's own. The fix: plan_shots.py gives each shot the pause in front of it; the builder anchors every boundary to the words. scripts/common/repair_drift.py re-plans and rebuilds an episode. This file documents one re-plan, one re-build, and a clean gate.

The QA gate reliability — what actually happened

One pass. Two findings. Both false positives.

Same pattern as Speedmaster: the gate misreads period artwork as modern, and treats woodblock prints as "infographics." No real defects were found in the rebuilt MP4. Open the frame before acting on any "modern" finding.
