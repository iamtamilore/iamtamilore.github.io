Image QA Gate

Catches the moment an AI-generated model stops being the same person. A Siamese network over frozen ResNet50 features, judged against two baselines: pixel difference scored F1 0.59, kNN 0.26, the Siamese 0.90. 90 sealed test pairs. The cutoff was tuned on validation data only, never on the test set.

The thing nobody else publishes

A blinded evaluation, run on my own output.
blind, seeded, human-rated
80 generated garments, 4 conditions, rated by people who cannot see which condition produced what

80 images, four conditions, a frozen rubric

Everyone building on image models shows you the good outputs. So I ran the opposite test. Eighty garment images across four generation conditions, presented to human raters in a seeded shuffle, with condition and filenames masked and the rubric frozen before anyone rated anything. The rubric gates on one question first: is this image clear enough to judge at all. If the answer is no, the rest is skipped rather than guessed.

The interesting part is not the score. It is that an automatic OCR score and a human judgement disagree often enough to matter, and the only way to find out which one is right is to ask a person who cannot see the pipeline.
